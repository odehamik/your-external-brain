# Your External Brain

You have acquired a second brain. Please do not give it jurisdiction over the first.

A small, customizable folder for keeping work where you can find it and deciding how an AI participates. Built for an Indigenous AI gathering through Maya Chacaby’s work. Participants bring their own relations and governing protocols.

**You only need one ZIP.** On GitHub, open the ZIP link and use “Download raw file” or the download icon. If GitHub is unavailable, your facilitator can send you the same ZIP directly. You do not need Git, a terminal, or the whole repository.

## Begin

1. Download and extract your chosen ZIP.
2. Open **00 OPEN ME FIRST.txt**. It tells you how to copy **START-HERE.txt** into a new conversation in your AI app.
3. Send the starter text. The AI should welcome you, ask one useful setup question, and wait for your answer.

If it summarizes the instructions, say: **“Begin guiding me now. Give me the first step and wait.”**

You can use your own words. “I’m lost” is a complete request. Formal is fine too; you owe the machine neither professionalism nor intimacy.

The first conversation helps with your actual device, app, permissions, and one note you choose. It can start with an empty note. No fictional exercise, cultural disclosure, paid upgrade, or installed agent is required. Keep personal and community material out until its use in that tool is permitted.

## Inside the folder

A landing for permitted files, context you choose to keep, projects and publications, relation and boundary instructions, an optional PM, reusable skills, and a Muck archive for selected old conversations. The guide introduces these when useful; they are not a reading assignment.

The handshake locates the machine’s material and political relations and gives responsibilities consequences in the next action. Participants can change its wording. The kit does not prescribe one Indigenous ceremony, grant cultural authority to an AI, or make privacy guarantees through folder names.
