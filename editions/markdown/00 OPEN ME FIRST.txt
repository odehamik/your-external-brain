YOUR EXTERNAL BRAIN — BEGIN HERE

You do not have to become formal to speak to this machine.

Need an AI app or a way to open this folder first? Open README.md in the Markdown edition or README.txt in the Plain Text edition. “Get the tools ready” has official download links, login steps, Obsidian settings, and the plain-text route. Already ready? Begin below.

1. Open START-HERE.txt in this folder. Select and copy all the text inside it.
2. Open a new conversation in the AI app you use. Paste the text into the message box and send it.
3. The AI should begin helping you set up, one step at a time. Answer in your own words. “I do not know” is a usable answer.

Copy the words INSIDE START-HERE.txt, not just its filename. The AI cannot read a file on your device merely because you have told it the file exists.

If it summarizes the instructions instead of beginning, say:
“Begin guiding me now. Give me the first step and wait.”

If it gives you too much, say:
“One thing at a time. What do I do right now?”

You do not need to upload the whole folder. Keep personal and community material out of the conversation while you get set up. The guide can begin using only the starter text.

If this is still a ZIP, extract or unzip it first. On a shared device, choose somewhere you are allowed to keep your copy. A facilitator can help with opening and copying; you never need to hand over your password.

Use the Plain Text folder OR the Markdown folder as your working copy. You do not have to maintain both. START-HERE.txt is ordinary text in both editions.

No AI app available right now? You can still open README and explore the folder with a person. The instructions will be here when you return.
