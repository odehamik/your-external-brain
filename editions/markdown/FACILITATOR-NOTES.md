# Facilitator notes — Guided entry edition

Begin by having each participant open 00 OPEN ME FIRST.txt. Help them copy START-HERE.txt into a new AI conversation and send it. This is the first actual action; the participant does not need to read the whole README or understand the folder beforehand.

The expected response is a short relational opening and one relevant setup question, then a wait. If the model reviews the prompt, the recovery line is: “Begin guiding me now. Give me the first step and wait.” If it already knows the device, it should use that answer. A person can adapt the handshake, request quiet language, or begin with a practical problem without having to justify that choice.

Participants can be informal, formal, uncertain, reserved, or funny. Avoid teaching that there is a correct prompting voice. Demonstrate that “I’m lost” is sufficient to change the next response. The participant’s interruption needs to work.

This revision follows Maya’s report that a participant using the Plain Text folder could not get the LLM to start guiding setup. The exact failed transcript was not inspected. The diagnosis comes from that report and the entry instructions, not a replay of the participant’s session.


The opening is small: open one edition, find the relevant settings if wanted, and begin with something the participant is allowed to use or an empty note. FIRST-TIME.md carries that route. No fictional exercise, invented project, or archive test is included. The guide’s short response examples remain in MACHINE-ENTRY as instructions about conduct; participants are not asked to perform them.

Participants need not complete the folder, choose skills, disclose cultural practices, share screens, upload an archive, buy software, or activate agents. Exploring, declining, and leaving a thought open are valid ways to participate. Keep explanations tied to what someone is trying to do.

## Before people arrive

Keep the Plain Text ZIP available as the simple starting edition and Markdown as an option. Explain that the ZIP needs extracting. Avoid distributing both as two folders people must maintain. Offer a device-appropriate way to receive files privately; do not gather account credentials. Have a way to read the welcome together or on paper if downloads or connectivity fail.

Invite access preferences without diagnoses: one step at a time, larger text, keyboard instructions, screen-reader-friendly prose, quiet wording, a human reader, breaks, or help in a language the participant chooses. Diagrams need text equivalents. Voice is optional and may involve recording/transfer; never require it. The model must flag uncertainty in translation, particularly cultural language, and leave adjudication with people who carry that authority.

A participant using a shared device should choose where their copy lives and whether it will remain there. Avoid public projection of account settings or personal files. If a file needs to stay outside AI systems, settings troubleshooting is not an exception.

## During the gathering

Let the actual app determine the instructions. A folder-capable tool, attachment chat, and ordinary text chat need different routes. SETTINGS-AND-STUCK.md includes manual alternatives, and PROVIDER-SETTINGS.md has dated official sources. Training opt-out, retention, memory, file permissions, and sharing are separate. Unknown settings remain unknown. A person can continue with an empty note while waiting for administrator help.

Help each person find whatever they choose to keep. Opening the actual saved file is ordinary assistance, not an assessment. If the model only wrote text in chat, help save it. At leaving time, offer a short place-to-return note; no need to export the whole conversation.

More files, reusable procedures, and additional agents are available through MAKE-IT-YOURS.md when asked for. None is installed or running just because its description exists. Keep tomorrow’s beginning small without making the rest inaccessible.

## What this review establishes

The package is written for human agency over storage and the machine’s participation. The approved welcome and relation ground are preserved. CARE and specifically First Nations OCAP® remain distinct; no framework is presented as machine-certified compliance. No private MythOS material or participant data is included.

Document review and link/archive checks can establish that the files are packaged and their routes exist. They cannot establish every device’s accessibility, a provider’s real account settings, model obedience, or security of a participant’s storage. No fresh-model behavior test has been performed. Do not describe those as passed.

For later work involving protected data or unattended jobs, the relevant authority and actual capability checks still matter. Removing tomorrow’s fictional exercise does not certify those systems. Keep such work manual and scoped until suitable handling is established.

The wording does not teach a universal Indigenous cadence. Follow the particular person and their language, humour, refusals, and request for practical action. A respectful guide changes what it does when corrected. It does not make the participant listen to an essay about how respectful the correction has made it.
