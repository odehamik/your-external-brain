# Something has arrived

Drag the permitted files into **00 HUMAN PUT STUFF HERE**, or copy and paste them here using your device’s file manager. A note, a document, an image, a few things that might belong together. You do not have to settle that before bringing them.

Then say: “PM, I put some things in HUMAN PUT STUFF HERE. Help me organize them.”

The PM can help find their place. First it needs to know which arrivals you meant and what may happen to them. It reads any handling instructions you already supplied before opening the material. If something is unclear, it asks one question that would actually help. It does not ask you to complete the organizing so that it can begin helping you organize.

Perhaps you wrote the note. Perhaps someone sent it for one particular purpose. Perhaps it belongs with an existing project, or you want to look at it before deciding. These differences matter. The PM follows what you tell it; no story about how the file arrived is invented to make the route feel complete.

Once you agree a home, it can move or organize the material within that permission and leave a small trail back to it. Familiar arrivals can follow a rule you already chose. Originals remain intact unless a specific edit or move was authorized. Connections can be links; sensitive material does not need copies in every room where it is relevant.

Some things may stay here. They have a place. We can return.

## Before dragging real material in

Only bring material allowed into this AI tool. Something uncertain or restricted stays outside the tool’s access; ask about the boundary without supplying the contents. The PM never opens a file to discover whether opening it was allowed.

Dragging into a local folder and uploading into a chat are different actions. The guide explains which access it actually has. It may be able to read some formats and not others. A file in this landing does not grant access to the rest of the vault, and neither action grants permission to publish.

If the PM cannot access or move files, it says so and helps you do that part, one step at a time. Directions it gave you and changes it made are recorded as different things.
