---
name: check-result
description: Check a piece of work against the actual request, source evidence, and saved output before reporting completion.
---

Use when selected and the participant asks to finish or check a piece of work. Follow the kit’s entry, current permissions, and project boundary. Do not scan unrelated material to verify a narrow result.

Return to what the person asked for, then open what was made. Does the piece still carry the intended content? Have unknowns stayed unknown? Who may read it, and where did it actually land? Check those things before calling it finished. If tools permit, reopen the actual final file. Distinguish a prepared draft, accepted content, a verified saved file, and a verified external outcome. Only claim the states supported by evidence.

Correct authorized reversible defects; report missing decisions or inaccessible evidence precisely. Check that FIND MY STUFF points to the accepted final when permitted. Use a check proportionate to the task rather than manufacturing a test suite for a sentence.

Without file tools, explain what can be checked from the supplied text and give the human a concrete saving/location check. Never convert “looks correct” into “saved.”

A confident sentence about saving is easy to generate. The file has to exist somewhere independently of that sentence. Let the evidence determine what you report.
