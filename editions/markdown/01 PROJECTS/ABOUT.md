# Where this work can live

How would you look for it again?

Start there. The guide helps make a room you recognize, using the name and connections you choose. Maya’s project template stays in Maya’s vault. This room need not inherit its habits.

You may organize around a community purpose, a set of responsibilities, activities, things being made, stages, or a funder’s requirements. More than one route can be useful. Keep what the community asks and what the funder asks separately visible. Where they disagree, the disagreement belongs in the record; changing the heading will not resolve it.

For a small project, ABOUT.md, a boundary note, and Finished/ may be enough. Add rooms as the work needs them. A structure can grow without first being described in its entirety.

Ask only what matters now: what is the person trying to do, and who can decide what happens to the material? A purpose can be provisional. Permission to use protected material cannot be guessed while the purpose is taking shape.

Before protected data enters, agree the permitted tools and access. Record the handling rule outside the protected content. Where names need changing, agree whether pseudonyms, redaction, aggregation, or no AI access is appropriate. Never deanonymize. An available file is not evidence of consent.

With authorization, use `_Machine/DATA-HANDLING.md` to make the project’s rule something the guide can follow at arrival, in drafts, summaries, searches, logs, exports, and other models. Before relying on it for protected material, have its behavior checked without exposing that material; this is later governed setup, not a required workshop exercise. The machine flags what it can see; the proper human authority decides what is acceptable.

If the work changes, come back to the room. See what still fits.
