## How things leave

A list that only grows turns into furniture. Things leave as well as arrive.

- **Done, or let go.** Either one closes the item. Tick it and move it to *Closed* below with the date. Letting something go needs no reason.
- **Waiting on someone else.** Move it to *Waiting on others*, without a checkbox. Nothing is owed by you until it comes back.
- **The machine proposes; you close.** The PM may say something looks finished, stale, or past its date. Only you tick it.
- **When the list stops fitting on one screen,** ask the PM for a short list of items that look done or past their date, and decide those together.
