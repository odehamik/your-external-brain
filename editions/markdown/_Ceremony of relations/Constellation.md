# Constellation

One note brings you back to another. A question in an old conversation changes how you read something you made today. If you want to keep that connection visible, it can have a place here—with its sources, and with room for you to read it differently later.

You do not have to catalogue your relatives to operate a computer.

Land, non-human kin, communities, collaborators, work, and the machine’s responsibilities may be named here only as you choose and have authority to share. Leaving them unnamed does not make them absent. Cultural knowledge is not required to fill out this page.

## Connections you choose

No personal relations or historical sessions have been added. The guide proposes a short connection with its source and why it matters; you can accept, change, or decline it. Keep sensitive connections project-local if this general map would reveal them too widely. Sometimes even a link title reveals too much. In that case, leave no general pointer.

Historical links remain historical. A prior conversation cannot rewrite today’s boundaries. This is a text map first; a visual graph is optional and needs a readable text equivalent.
