# Whose authority operates here?

CARE means **Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, and Ethics**. GIDA centres people, purpose, and Indigenous self-determination in data governance. [GIDA: CARE Principles](https://www.gida-global.org/careprinciples).

OCAP® means **ownership, control, access, and possession**. FNIGC identifies these as specifically First Nations principles; their application follows the respective Nation’s worldview and protocols. They are not a universal assignment for every Indigenous participant. OCAP® is a registered trademark of the First Nations Information Governance Centre (FNIGC). [FNIGC: OCAP®](https://fnigc.ca/ocap-training/).

## Use the actual governing instructions

The following are this kit’s proposed practical questions, not an official checklist, certification, or substitute for either source:

- Who determines what benefit this work should bring, and who bears its costs?
- Who can authorize this particular use, tool, audience, and destination?
- What responsibilities continue after an output is made, including correction, return, access, and withdrawal?
- What harms or contested decisions require the affected people’s process rather than a model’s conclusion?
- Where may material physically reside, and who can actually control and access those copies?

Ask only what the current task needs. Record operational rules the authorized people provide, not protected teachings. Local protocols can require that material never enter AI. Unclear collective authority is held; an individual upload does not resolve it.

For this international group, ask which Nation/community authorities and protocols govern the specific work. CARE, OCAP®, or another framework can inform that conversation where applicable. Do not replace those authorities with a generic global policy, or assert that a local folder satisfies them.

## Consequences in the work

The PM applies approved rules at intake, skill selection, drafting, archiving, indexing, delegation, and sharing. If a tool cannot meet a required condition, keep the material out and offer a permitted alternative. Preserve credit where required rather than automatically stripping names. Refusal, non-use, and keeping knowledge outside the system can be successful outcomes.

No “CARE compliant,” “OCAP® certified,” or “decolonized” badge is awarded by the machine. The work is answerable through the relevant human processes. This folder does not grade its own politics and print a certificate.
