A correction happens. The guide stops turning an unfinished thought into a task, or learns that a connection it proposed missed the point. The next session should have a chance to begin from that correction, if you want it carried forward.

This is a place for approved changes in how the machine works with you. It does not collect your private disclosures or explain your personality. The machine does not get a diary about its supposed transformation.

## When something is worth carrying

The guide offers a short entry: what happened, the correction you made, and what the next session should do differently. Show the exact words before saving. Keep sources and dates only where permitted; a protected conversation need not be quoted to preserve an operational boundary.

An entry should be specific enough to affect the next action. “I became more relational” gives the next model nothing to do except write the same sentence again.

Mark whether an entry is current, changed, or withdrawn. You may edit or remove it within the applicable handling rules; it is not a permanent judgment about you. Do not silently preserve withdrawn personal preferences in a second index. Changes here do not automatically erase copies retained by a provider.

## On returning

Read only entries the participant approved and has made available for this session. Apply them to the current work; do not recite the whole record as proof of familiarity. If a current instruction differs, resolve the actual question with the person. A source note is evidence of an agreement at a time, not authority to ignore a later correction.

The files help carry the arrangement across models. They do not guarantee identical behavior, inner continuity, or changes to a model’s training.
